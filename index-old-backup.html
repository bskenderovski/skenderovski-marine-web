About Skenderovski Marine

Two decades of putting Macedonia on the water

In 2007, Skenderovski Marine earned the rights to sell and service Mercury MerCruiser and Mercury Outboards — the foundation of everything we've built since. That single partnership grew into a full-service marine house representing Sea Ray, Jeanneau, Quicksilver, Marine Aluminium and Vesta Trailers.

Today, our family-run team in Skopje guides clients from first boat to lifelong upkeep, backed by certified Mercury technicians, genuine parts, and a transport division that moves vessels safely across the region.
